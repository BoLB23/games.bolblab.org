# FlappyMike Art System and Regeneration Prompts

## Canonical style

FlappyMike uses a playful modern 2D cartoon style for a polished indie browser arcade game: clean silhouettes, crisp shapes, warm humor, moderate detail, subtle hand-drawn texture, and soft upper-left summer daylight. All scenery is strict side view and keeps the center flight corridor lower-contrast than silhouettes and collision edges.

Palette:

- sky blue `#78b8dd` / `#86cce8`
- cloud cream `#fff7df`
- Philadelphia brick `#9d5145`, mortar `#633a35`
- industrial slate `#667479`
- transition sage `#71826c`
- Lancaster field `#79a759`, deep green `#47763e`
- barn red `#a94539`, hay gold `#d6aa4a`
- outline charcoal `#342f2d`
- FlappyMike chestnut `#a85020`, golden wing `#d88931`, cream belly `#f3dca9`

Canonical reference: `assets/flappymike/source/flappymike_reference.png`. Every player regeneration must use it as an identity reference. Preserve the exact bald crown, charcoal glasses, curled dark mustache, warm chestnut/golden body, cream belly, beak, feet, tail, proportions, right-facing profile, and upper-left lighting.

Global negative constraints: no Angry Birds resemblance; no existing character imitation; no commercial or municipal logos; no watermarks; no accidental text; no three-quarter or top-down view; no photorealism; no pixel art; no corporate clip-art; no extra wings or limbs; no inconsistent lighting; no opaque background on cutouts; no edge halos.

Only the 512×640 RGBA PNG plus Phaser JSON atlas is shipped from `public/`. Source prompt suffix: “Perfectly flat solid `#00ff00` background, no shadows, gradients, texture, floor, or green in the character; entire bird centered with generous padding.”

Master character prompt:

> Create an original 2D side-view cartoon game character named FlappyMike, flying right. He is a small round middle-aged-ish comedic bird with a bald crown, thick charcoal hipster glasses, a prominent curled dark mustache, expressive eyes and eyebrows, a chestnut and muted-golden body, cream belly, small wing, short tail feathers, and tiny tucked feet. Mildly smug, determined, lovable. Playful modern illustrated indie arcade style, bold original silhouette, subtle hand-drawn texture, crisp readable forms, moderate detail, bright soft summer daylight. Entire bird visible and readable at 64–96 px. No text, props, scenery, logos, watermark, or existing-character resemblance.

## QA checklist

- Compare every player frame against the canonical reference at both 128 px and the in-game 70 px size.
- Verify alpha corners, no green fringe, no clipped wing/tail, and no frame-size/body changes.
- Render each tileable texture three times side by side and inspect both seams.
- Confirm obstacle silhouettes remain secondary to the bird but have unambiguous gap edges.
- Check the city → outskirts → country sequence at transition progress 0, .2, .4, .6, .8, and 1.
- Test FIT scaling in landscape and portrait viewports, debug hitboxes, restart, object recycling, and a full city-to-country run.
